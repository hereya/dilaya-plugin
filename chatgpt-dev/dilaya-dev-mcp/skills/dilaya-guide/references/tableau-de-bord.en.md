# A live dashboard

Beyond a simple display inside the conversation, you can have a **real dashboard** — a rich visual panel (cards, charts, filters) that reflects your data.

## When it helps

- Following **indicators** at a glance (revenue, tasks, stock…).
- Having a **control screen** you reopen regularly.
- **Managing** from one place (seeing all your apps, acting on them).

## How to ask

> "In Dilaya, make me a **dashboard** of my business: orders this month, revenue, top products, with a chart."

The dashboard is designed, generated, and its **recipe is saved** so you can find it again and regenerate it later, even on another machine.

## Tip

If you want a dashboard to **manage all your apps in one place** (see them, put them online, tidy them away), ask for the **management console** — see "Manage all your apps".
