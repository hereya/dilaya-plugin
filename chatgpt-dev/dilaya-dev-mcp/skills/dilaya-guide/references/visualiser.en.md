# See your data properly

Instead of lines of text, your data can be shown as **neat cards, lists or tables**, right in the conversation.

## How to ask

> "In Dilaya, show my customers as **cards**, with the name large and the town underneath."

> "In Dilaya, make me a **table** of my tasks, sorted by due date."

> "In Dilaya, show a **visual summary** of my expenses by category."

## It stays alive

These displays are **reusable**: they are regenerated with fresh data when you ask again, without starting over. You can also make them **clickable** (click a card to see the detail).

## Two levels of display

- **In the conversation** (this tutorial) — quick, for you, while you talk.
- **On the web** — a real site other people open in their browser (see "Put your app online").

## Prompts to copy

> "In Dilaya, create a 'customers' view as cards and show it."

> "In Dilaya, a dashboard with: orders this month, revenue, and top 5 customers."
