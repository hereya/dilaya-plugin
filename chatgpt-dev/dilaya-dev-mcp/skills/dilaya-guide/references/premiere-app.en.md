# Create your first app

A Dilaya "app" is your software: it **keeps your information on records** and lets you find, filter and update them.

## The simplest way

Describe what you need to your assistant, in your own words. No technical vocabulary required — talk about **what you want to keep track of** and **what each record should hold**.

A prompt to copy:

> "With Dilaya, build me software to keep track of my **customers**. For each customer I want: name, email, phone, town, and the date we last spoke."

It will then: suggest a name, define the records and their fields, create the software, and confirm it is ready.

## Give all the useful detail

The more precise you are about **what to store**, the better. Think about:

- **What** you want to track (customers? products? tasks?)
- **Which details** per record (name, amount, date, status…)
- **The links** between things ("each order belongs to a customer")

## Prompts to copy

> "With Dilaya, a **recipe** notebook: name, servings, prep time, category, and the list of ingredients with quantities."

> "With Dilaya, an **expense** tracker: date, amount, category, payment method, note."

> "With Dilaya, a small **CRM**: companies, contacts (attached to a company), and interactions (call/email/meeting with a date)."

Once the software exists, you can go on: add data, look at it, or put it online (see the other tutorials).
