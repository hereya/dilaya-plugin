# Manage all your apps

Once you have several pieces of software, you can drive them **from one place**: a console showing each app with its state, and letting you act on it safely.

## What you see

**One card per app**: its name, its state (Online / Ready / Data only), how much information and how many users it holds, and its features (sign-in, Telegram, dashboards…).

## What you can do

- **Open** an app that is online, or copy a ready-made prompt to use it.
- **Put online** an app that is not published yet.
- **Tidy away (archive)** an app you no longer use — this is **reversible**, you can restore it.
- **Delete** for good (with a confirmation) an app you no longer need.

## How to ask

> "Set up the **management console** for my Dilaya apps."

The console is set up and you are given what you need to open it. You can also simply ask:

> "List all my Dilaya apps with their state."
