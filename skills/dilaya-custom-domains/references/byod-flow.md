## Flow

> **Bought through Dilaya? Nothing to add.** When the domain — or the ROOT it belongs to — was bought with `register-domain`, its DNS zone lives in Dilaya's account: `set-custom-domain` WRITES the records itself (`dns_managed_by_dilaya: true` + `managed_zone`) and `check-custom-domains` re-writes anything still missing on every run. That covers **subdomains added later** (`www.moncafe.fr` on a bought `moncafe.fr`), at any depth, and `redirect_to` hosts. Do NOT relay the records as an action list in that case — say the DNS is done and just re-run `check-custom-domains` until `active`. Everything below is the BYOD case: a domain the customer holds elsewhere.

1. `set-custom-domain({ schema, domain: "commandes.acme.com" })` → returns `dns_records` — the FULL list to add at the customer's DNS provider:
   - the **certificate validation** CNAME(s),
   - the **routing** record: a CNAME to the returned `distribution_domain` (an apex domain like `acme.com` needs ALIAS/ANAME support at the provider — flagged in the record's `note`),
   - the **email** records (DKIM TXT + return-path CNAME) unless you passed `email: false`.
   Relay these records to the user verbatim (name, type, value) and tell them to add ALL of them. They can be added in one pass, right away.
2. Once the user says the records are in place (DNS can take minutes to propagate), run `check-custom-domains({ schema })` — it promotes the domain live when the certificate validates, verifies the email DKIM, and **switches the app's mail sender AND its OTP (login) sender to `noreply@<domain>`** once verified (mail needs `enable-mail`; the OTP sender switches only if `enable-auth` was run). The switch also works on a later re-run with `schema` — the domain does not need to have just verified. When the app has SEVERAL email-verified domains, the **apex is preferred** (e.g. `cariaco.fr` over `www.cariaco.fr`). Re-run until every domain reports `active`. It also re-lists any DNS record still missing. If the app has passkeys (`enable-auth`), a domain going live becomes their **principal host**: the pool is re-bound and the report says so — passkeys registered on the previous host stop working and users register again after their next e-mail code (same on `remove-custom-domain`).
3. The app now serves at `https://<domain>/` with the SAME routes as the vanity host. Handlers that build links from `req.baseUrl` need no change.
