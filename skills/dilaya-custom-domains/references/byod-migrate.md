## Migrating a domain that is ALREADY live elsewhere

A domain currently serving a site on another CloudFront distribution (e.g. moving an existing site to Dilaya) is detected automatically: `set-custom-domain` then ALSO returns a `move_proof` TXT record and a `live_migration.steps` sequence — relay them verbatim. The flow: add the validation CNAME + move_proof TXT (no traffic impact), keep the old site serving while the certificate validates, then `check-custom-domains` moves the domain ATOMICALLY onto the Dilaya distribution (the old platform stops serving it at that instant) — tell the user to switch the routing CNAME immediately after, and warn that visitors with a cached DNS answer may see errors until their cache expires (the domain's DNS TTL): pick a quiet window. If a move is refused, the check reports the domain `pending` with a `move_note` + the missing record — fix and re-run.
