# Discover Dilaya

Dilaya lets you have **your own software, built around you** — without a developer — just by talking to your assistant. You describe what you do, it builds the software and Dilaya keeps it running.

## What you can build

- 📇 **A directory or a catalogue** — contacts, products, recipes, rentals… anything that fits on a record.
- 🗂️ **Software to run the work** — tasks, orders, appointments, stock, expenses.
- 🌐 **A website** real people use from their browser, with sign-in by email.
- 💬 **A Telegram bot** to check on your software, or drive it, from your phone.
- 🤖 **Automation** — a helper that does a job on its own at a fixed time (a summary every morning, a watch…).
- 📊 **A live dashboard** to see your figures at a glance.

## How it works, in practice

There is **nothing to install**: you connect Dilaya to your assistant once (see "Connect Dilaya to your assistant"), then you say what you want, in plain words. It does the rest: create the software, store your data, show it to you, and — if you want — put it online.

## What is left when the conversation closes

That is the difference from "doing the same thing with AI". A conversation produces a table, a document, a PDF — and you start again next month. Here, what you describe **becomes software**: it has an address, it keeps your data, it is still there tomorrow.

## Where to start

Just say what you want to do. For example:

> "With Dilaya, I want a notebook to keep track of my customers and their orders."

> "With Dilaya, help me list my recipes with their ingredients."

> "With Dilaya, I'd like software to manage the bookings of my venue."

You can also ask for a specific tutorial (see the menu below) or simply describe what you need — you will be given the steps in the context of Dilaya.
