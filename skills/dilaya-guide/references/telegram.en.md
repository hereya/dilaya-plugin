# Drive your app from Telegram

You can attach a **Telegram bot** to your software: you write to it, it answers — to read your data or trigger actions from your phone.

## What you need

- A **Telegram bot** (you will be shown how to create one in a minute via @BotFather, which gives you a "token").
- Your **Telegram id** (from @userinfobot) so you are the owner.

## Private by default

The bot only talks **to the people you allow**. You are the owner; you can add other people with roles (admin, member).

## What it is good for

- **Getting alerts**: "tell me on Telegram whenever a new order comes in".
- **Checking**: you write "how many tasks are overdue?" and the bot answers.
- **Acting**: "mark order 1024 as shipped".
- **Buttons**: the bot can offer buttons to tap (Yes/No, Confirm…) instead of typing.

## How to ask

> "In Dilaya, attach a **Telegram bot** to my app so I get an alert on every new order and can confirm them with a button."

You are guided through creating the bot and pasting the token safely (never in the conversation).
