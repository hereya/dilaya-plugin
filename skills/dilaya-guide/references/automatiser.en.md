# Automate tasks

You can hand recurring work to an **automatic helper** that runs **without you** — at a fixed time, or as soon as something happens.

## Two ways

- ⏰ **At a fixed time** — "every morning at 8, prepare a summary of yesterday's orders and send it to me".
- 🔔 **When something new happens** — a helper that reacts as soon as a message or a change arrives (answering on Telegram automatically, for instance).

## Examples

> "In Dilaya, every Monday, send me the week's **top 5 customers** on Telegram."

> "In Dilaya, as soon as a task goes past its due date, **tell me**."

> "In Dilaya, every evening, make a **summary backup** of the day's activity."

## How to ask

Just say what you want automated, **when**, and **how** to be told:

> "In Dilaya, set up a helper that, **every day at 6pm**, gives me a Telegram rundown of tomorrow's appointments."

The automation gets set up and you are told how to switch it on. Some run in the cloud (nothing to leave switched on at home), others on your own machine — the choice follows what you need, and it is explained to you.
