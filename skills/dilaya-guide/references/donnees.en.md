# Add and find your data

Once your software exists, you fill it and read it **by talking** — your assistant turns your requests into actions.

## Add information

> "In Dilaya, add the customer **Marie Dupont**, marie@example.com, in Lyon."

> "In Dilaya, record an expense of **€42** today, category travel."

## Import a lot at once

Already have a list (a file, a table)? Paste it or describe it:

> "Here are 30 products as a table, import them into my Dilaya catalogue."

## Search and filter

> "In Dilaya, show me the customers in **Lyon** last contacted more than 3 months ago."

> "In Dilaya, what are my **5 biggest expenses** this month?"

## Update or delete

> "In Dilaya, change Marie Dupont's phone to 06 12 34 56 78."

> "In Dilaya, mark order #1024 as **delivered**."

> "In Dilaya, delete the record for product **obsolete-123**."

## Tip

If you often repeat the same request (a weekly report, an export…), say so: it can be **saved into your software** and repeated with one word next time — even in another conversation.
