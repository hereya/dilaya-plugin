# Welcome to Dilaya 👋

Dilaya lets you have **your own software, built around you, without a developer** — just by talking. You describe what you do, and your assistant builds the software, stores your data, shows it to you, and can even put it online.

## What you can build

- 📇 A **directory / catalogue** (contacts, products, recipes…)
- 🗂️ **Software to run the work** (tasks, orders, appointments, expenses)
- 🌐 A **website** real people use, with sign-in by email
- 💬 A **Telegram bot** to drive your software from your phone
- 🤖 **Automation** (a summary every morning, a watch…)
- 📊 A **live dashboard** to see your figures

## Where to start

**Just tell me what you want to do, in your own words.** For example:

> "With Dilaya, I want a notebook to keep track of my customers and their orders."

> "With Dilaya, help me list my recipes with their ingredients."

> "With Dilaya, I'd like software to manage the bookings of my venue."

You can also **describe your goal** and I will map out the steps: the `dilaya-guide` skill.

## Available tutorials

- **Connect Dilaya to your assistant** — The one step you do once: link your Dilaya account to your assistant, on a computer or a phone.  
  _(ask for : the `brancher` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **Discover Dilaya** — What Dilaya is and what you can do with it, in one page.  
  _(ask for : the `decouvrir` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **Create your first app** — From scratch: describe what you need and get working software.  
  _(ask for : the `premiere-app` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **Add and find your data** — Fill your software, search, filter, update, import in bulk.  
  _(ask for : the `donnees` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **See your data properly** — Show your information as cards, lists or tables right in the conversation.  
  _(ask for : the `visualiser` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **Put your app online (website)** — A real site real people use, with sign-in by email.  
  _(ask for : the `en-ligne` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **Drive your app from Telegram** — Check on your software and act on it by message, from your phone.  
  _(ask for : the `telegram` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **Automate tasks** — A helper that works on its own, at a fixed time or when something new happens.  
  _(ask for : the `automatiser` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **A live dashboard** — A rich, shareable panel fed by your own data.  
  _(ask for : the `tableau-de-bord` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **Manage all your apps** — See all your software in one place and act on each one safely.  
  _(ask for : the `gerer-apps` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **The app catalogue** — Install a ready-made app in minutes, or share your own.  
  _(ask for : the `catalogue` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_
- **Your subscription and your card** — Where to put your card, change it, read your invoices, and what happens when the trial ends.  
  _(ask for : the `abonnement` tutorial of the `dilaya-guide` skill)_

Tell me what you need, or pick a tutorial — I will take care of the rest. 🚀
