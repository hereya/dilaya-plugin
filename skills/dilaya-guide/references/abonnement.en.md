# Your subscription, your card and your invoices

Your Dilaya space starts with a **free trial, no card** — 14 days by default; the exact date is shown on your subscription page. Here is where to put your card when you want to carry on, and what happens if you do nothing.

## Where to put your card

1. Go to **https://dilaya.eu/my-organization** and sign in: you enter your email, you receive a **6-digit code** by email, and that is it — there is no password (you can then save a passkey to be quicker next time).
2. In the **"Subscription"** section, click **"Add a payment method"**.
3. You land on a **Stripe** payment page: your bank details go straight to them, Dilaya never sees your card number.

**Only the owner of the space sees that button.** If you were invited as a member, the person who owns the space takes care of it — the page tells you so.

## Changing your card, your invoices, cancelling

Once the card is saved, the same page opens **"Manage my billing"**, at Stripe: there you **change your card**, **download your invoices** and **cancel** if you wish. None of that goes through us.

## If you do nothing

You get an email reminder **7 days, 3 days and 1 day** before the trial ends, then on the day itself. After that day your space is **paused**: your software stops answering in the conversation.

**Nothing is deleted.** Your software, your data, your files and your team stay exactly where they are, and everything comes back as it was as soon as a payment method is saved.

## Adding people

Extra seats for your team are added from that same page, once the card is saved. The price is shown there before you confirm.

## In short

> Everything happens at **https://dilaya.eu/my-organization**, with the email address that owns the space.
